# The Meeting About the Meeting

Entry for [The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c) — one short line about office life, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The joke

> My manager said the meeting could have been an email, then sent a calendar invite for a meeting to discuss why the meeting could have been an email.

148 characters. The setup is the most universal office complaint — the meeting that could have been an email — and the turn is recursive: the manager schedules a meeting about the meeting that could have been an email. Punchline lands last, every word earns its place, and the joke is fresh rather than a retread.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
